## The project

A volunteer student team with SiloamXperience is rebuilding the website of a school,
working from a product requirements document and Figma designs. The site is still
being built and is not public yet.

## My role

I lead the three-person Platform & Quality team:

- Plan and split the work each week and write clear, checkable tasks for teammates.
- Keep changes on focused branches with small, reviewable diffs.
- Check every change against the design and written acceptance criteria: theme
  checks, mobile and keyboard checks, and regression checks on existing pages.
- Look after the build and deployment pipeline with the tech lead.

Rook & Ruin

Play chess as White. A capture is only a challenge, and the duel type is rolled each time: a first-person shootout, a race, a platform brawl or a quick minigame. Each piece has its own weapon and stats. There is no check. A King has to be shot.

How to play
  • Board: click a white piece, then a highlighted square. A red square is a capture and starts a duel. There is no check; a King must lose a duel.
  • Each duel screen names the duel type and its controls before you press Fight. Black gets sharper every time you win a duel.
  • Shootout: WASD, mouse, R reloads, right mouse aims. Each piece has its own gun.
  • Race: W accelerates, S brakes, A and D steer, hold Space to handbrake into a drift. A long drift gives a boost when you straighten up.
  • Brawl: A and D move, W or Space jumps twice, J or click hits, K or right click hits hard, Shift dashes. Knock them off the screen twice.
  • Deflector: W and S or the mouse move your paddle. First to three.
  • Light trails: steer with WASD or arrows. Do not touch any trail or wall.
  • Ring push: WASD to move, Space to charge. Shove them out of the ring.
  • Pattern lock: watch the pads light up, then repeat with arrow keys or clicks.
  • Slag fall: A and D dodge the falling slag. Three hits and you are out.
  • Quickdraw, Target rush and Tug of war use Space or the mouse.
  • Every piece has an ability on E in every duel except the shootout: Pawn Shield wall, Knight Gallop, Bishop Hex, Rook Ram, Queen Dominion, King Decree. Black uses them too.
  • P or Esc pauses any duel. From the pause screen you can forfeit or leave to the main menu.

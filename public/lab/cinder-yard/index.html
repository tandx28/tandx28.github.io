How to play
  • Clear each wave. After a wave, the armoury lets you take one perk and spend skill points.
  • Guns: 1 carbine for most fights, 2 scattergun up close, 3 longrifle with right mouse to scope, 4 hand cannon for heavy single shots.
  • Powers recharge on their own. Q blinks you out of danger, E stuns a crowd, C slows every frame, G throws a grenade that bounces.
  • Shoot red barrels near enemies. Stay off glowing floor vents when they flash.
  • Lancers charge a red beam that always hits. Break line of sight or stun them before it fires.
  • Frames flank, throw grenades into cover and rush when you reload. Keep moving.
  • Horror mode: the lights are out, you have a torch, and Husks hunt you by smell. Listen for the heartbeat. It speeds up as they close in.
